Match Results

Switzerland 0-0 Colombia

Both teams displayed strong defensive organization and tactical discipline, leading to a cagey affair with limited clear-cut chances. Switzerland aimed for clinical finishing, while Colombia relied on a robust defensive platform, resulting in a goalless draw after extra time.
