👥Squad & Fitness Status

Core European Players Available

Ten players from reigning Czech champions Slavia Prague form the squad backbone, with Souček and Wolves defender Ladislav Krejčí bringing Premier League experience, while Bayer Leverkusen striker Patrik Schick leads the line. Stalwarts such as Vladimír Coufal, Tomáš Souček, Vladimír Darida, and Patrik Schick each have amassed over 50 international caps.
Key Players:
  • Patrik Schick (Bayer Leverkusen)
  • Tomáš Souček (West Ham)
  • Ladislav Krejčí (Wolves, captain)
  • Pavel Šulc (Olympique Lyon)

Adam Hložek Recovery Confirmed

Adam Hložek arrives as one of the nation's most exciting prospects despite a season hampered by injury, having made a remarkable recovery at age 23. No major injury absences reported for the final squad named on May 31.

Schick Fitness Paramount

Star attacker Patrik Schick is very fragile with injuries and illnesses, but must perform. He finished the 2025/26 season in good shape, scoring 16 Bundesliga goals. His health directly impacts offensive threat.

📊Recent Form & Qualification

Playoff Heroics Secure World Cup Return

Czech Republic qualified through UEFA playoffs in a campaign defined by resilience, overturning a 2-0 deficit against Ireland to reach penalties in the semifinals, then defeating Denmark in another dramatic shootout after a 2-2 draw. This was their first appearance on the World Cup stage in 20 years.

Pre-Tournament Friendlies Show Confidence

Czechia defeated Kosovo 2-1 on May 31 in a friendly, building momentum before the tournament. They face Guatemala on June 4 and South Korea on June 11 in Guadalajara for their group stage opener.

Qualifying Strength via Discipline

In UEFA qualifying, Czech Republic finished second in Group L behind Croatia, winning five of eight games with both defeats coming away—5-1 at Croatia and 2-1 in the Faroe Islands. The team showed resilience despite tough away performances.

⚔️Tactical Profile & Philosophy

Koubek's Structure-First Approach

Coach Miroslav Koubek at age 75 has built a side with clear philosophy: defend, endure and strike at the right moment, prioritizing structure and efficiency over possession. Koubek's Czechia side is organised, physical and flexible, moving away from the 4-2-3-1 to favour a fluid 3-4-2-1 system that often resembles a back five out of possession.
Formation:
3-4-2-1 (or back 5 in transition)

Defensive Resilience is Foundation

The fundamental philosophy of the Czech team is to avoid unforced errors at the back, as a really solid defence is key to success. Set-piece organisation and compact defensive shape are hallmarks of Koubek's preparation.

Efficient Attack & Schick Focal Point

Patrik Schick is the team's main attacking threat; his ability to convert limited chances makes him essential in a system that values efficiency over volume. Pavel Šulc is a dynamic attacking midfielder, having recently moved to Ligue 1 where he netted 11 goals and provided 3 assists in 2025/26.

🏆Group Stage Outlook

Group A Composition & Fixtures

Miroslav Koubek's side faces South Korea, South Africa and Mexico in Group A. Czechia open against South Korea in Guadalajara, then meet South Africa in Atlanta before facing co-hosts Mexico at the Estadio Azteca. All three opponents present distinct tactical challenges.

Playable Group but Travel Demands

Czechia will travel the fourth most miles among all 48 teams between their base in Dallas and match cities, with extreme heat and high altitude potentially affecting performance. The group features Mexico, South Korea and South Africa—not simple but playable, with one win and one draw potentially enough to reach Round of 32.

Breakthrough Opportunity After 20 Years

Head coach Miroslav Koubek named his final 26 players on May 31, with Wolves defender Ladislav Krejčí as captain. At 74, Koubek is the oldest manager at the 2026 tournament. The team enters aiming to advance past the group stage, something they failed to achieve in their only appearance as an independent nation in 2006.

🎯Match Predictions & Expectations

South Korea Test: Discipline vs. Pace

South Korea vs Czech Republic Group A campaign begins Thursday in Guadalajara. Predicted Czech XI shows defensive focus with a 3-4-2-1 shape. Schick and Šulc will need to exploit transitions against Korea's high-pressing style.

Success Scenario: Solid Defence, Clinical Attack

Czechia will need Schick at his best to get out of a difficult group. He may not be the most explosive striker, but is one of the most intelligent and efficient—he is the player who gives Czechia belief they can punch above their weight. Limiting errors and converting chances will be decisive.

Realistic Progression Odds

The European side enters with tactical discipline, resilience and efficiency; though not favourites, they are a dangerous opponent capable of competing through structure and game intelligence. One point from Korea/South Africa plus a steal vs Mexico could advance them; defeat to all three would be disappointing given group strength.
