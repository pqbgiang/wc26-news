Is Bosnia and Herzegovina Match-Ready?

👥Squad Status & Key Players

Full 26-Man Squad Confirmed

Bosnia named 26 players with breakdown of three goalkeepers, eight defenders, ten midfielders, and five forwards. Bosnia was the first of 48 nations to publish a full roster for the tournament.

Edin Džeko Leads Despite Age

Edin Džeko, 40 years old and playing for Schalke 04, showed he still has what it takes with six goals in Bosnia's World Cup qualifying campaign. Sead Kolašinac is the only other member of the 2014 World Cup squad alongside Džeko and plays for Atalanta in Serie A.
Key Players:
  • Edin Džeko (Captain)
  • Sead Kolašinac
  • Esmir Bajraktarević

Youth Movement Emerging

Five players are under 23 including Kerim Alajbegović at 18 years old who plays for RB Salzburg. Esmir Bajraktarević, the 21-year-old PSV Eindhoven winger, scored the winning penalty in the playoff final against Italy.

⚕️Injury Concerns

Džeko Shoulder Injury: Race Against Time

Džeko suffered a shoulder injury late in the Italy playoff final and faces a race against time to recover before the tournament with coach Barbarez saying 'he does not have much time'. Bosnia opens against co-host Canada on June 12 in Toronto, then faces Switzerland in Los Angeles and finishes against Qatar in Seattle on June 24.

Overall Squad Health Status

No additional major injuries reported in squad beyond Džeko's shoulder concern. Bosnia have been in a period of stalemates recently, winning 0 of their last 5 matches and scoring 4 goals while conceding 4 during warm-up period.

📊Recent Form & Momentum

Dramatic Playoff Qualification

In the playoffs, they defeated both Wales and Italy on penalties, qualifying for the 2026 World Cup and marking their second appearance as an independent nation. In a high-profile 1-1 against Italy settled by penalties, Bosnia controlled 65 percent of the ball, attempted 30 shots and put 11 on target, while Italy managed 9 shots and 3 on target.

Strong Qualifying Campaign Data

In World Cup 2026 qualifiers, Edin Džeko posted top Sofascore Rating at 7.48, followed by Kerim Alajbegović at 7.39 and Nikola Katić at 7.33; Džeko led the scoring with 6 goals, Haris Tabaković added 3. Recent friendlies saw 0-0 draw with North Macedonia and 1-1 draw with Panama.

🎯Tactical Setup & Game Plan

Attacking Depth & Flexibility

Bosnia's biggest strength is their attacking depth with multiple forwards and creative midfielders, allowing them to play flexible and direct football and score goals quickly. Dominating possession and chance creation against elite opponents showcases the team's depth with impact from both veterans and fresh faces.

Defensive Vulnerability

Defensive consistency remains a concern for Bosnia. Defensively, they have been solid, conceding an average of 0.8 goals per game recently.

Coaching Leadership & Structure

The reset began on 19 April 2024, when former captain Sergej Barbarez took over as head coach and World Cup 2014 skipper Emir Spahić became sporting director, surrounding themselves with familiar faces from the national shirt. Despite having zero previous senior management experience before taking the job, Barbarez led Bosnia to their most successful World Cup qualifying campaign in years.

⚽Group B Opponents & Matchups

Canada Opener (June 12, Toronto)

The opener against co-hosts Canada in Toronto is the toughest test on home soil. Bosnia will play their next match against Canada on June 12, 2026.

Switzerland Matchup (June 18, Los Angeles)

The Switzerland match is in Los Angeles. Switzerland is a settled first-choice XI that has carried the team through a perfect qualifying campaign and two recent tournament knockout runs.

Qatar Finale (June 24, Seattle)

The final group game is against Qatar in Seattle. Bosnia face Canada, Switzerland and Qatar in Group B.

🏆Tournament Outlook & Predictions

Historic Opportunity for Advancement

Now Barbarez's side will be looking to progress into the round of 16 for the first time, 12 years after making their tournament debut. Group B is open and the Dragons have a real chance to reach the knockout stage for the first time.

Unpredictable Threat in Tight Matches

What makes Bosnia dangerous is their unpredictability; although they are not always consistent, but when they find the pace, they can score quickly and put even stronger teams under pressure. For Bosnia, this tournament is not just about participation. It's about proving they belong.

Critical Path Dependency on Džeko Recovery

Bosnia's tournament success hinges on Edin Džeko's shoulder recovery and availability. Džeko faces a race against time to get his shoulder healed with coach Barbarez hoping 'he does not have much time'.
