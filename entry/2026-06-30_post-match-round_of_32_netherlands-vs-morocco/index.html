Match Results

Netherlands 1-1 (PSO 2-3) Morocco

The Netherlands adopted a more defensive approach with a five-man defense, initially taking the lead through Gakpo but then inviting pressure. Morocco consistently pushed forward, dominating possession in extra time and ultimately finding a late equalizer through persistent attacking.
