Match Summary

The Netherlands secured a dominant 5-1 victory over Sweden in Houston, with Brian Brobbey and Cody Gakpo each scoring twice, significantly boosting their position in Group F. This emphatic win, combined with Japan's 4-0 victory over Tunisia, has reshaped the group standings, placing the Netherlands and Japan at the top with four points each.
