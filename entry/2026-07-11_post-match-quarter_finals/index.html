France Dominates Morocco, Secures Semifinal Berth

France advanced to the semifinals with a commanding 2-0 victory over Morocco. Kylian Mbappe and Ousmane Dembele were on target, showcasing France's formidable attacking power and solidifying their status as tournament favorites.

Mbappe and Dembele Lead French Charge

Kylian Mbappe and Ousmane Dembele delivered the goals that sealed France's progression. Their clinical finishing and relentless pressure proved too much for a resilient Moroccan side, who fought hard but ultimately couldn't breach the French defense.

Spain Edges Belgium in Late Drama

Spain secured their spot in the semifinals with a dramatic 2-1 win against Belgium, decided by an 88th-minute goal. Fabian Ruiz opened the scoring for Spain, with Charles De Ketelaere equalizing for Belgium before Mikel Merino's late heroics.

Merino's Late Strike Sinks Resilient Belgium

Mikel Merino's decisive goal in the 88th minute, following a rebound from Pau Cubarsi's shot, broke Belgian hearts and sent Spain through. Belgium battled fiercely, even after losing goalkeeper Thibaut Courtois to injury, and ended Unai Simón's record 650-minute shutout streak.

Road to Glory: Semifinalists Emerge

France's Dominance Signals Title Intent

France has showcased exceptional form throughout the tournament, culminating in a confident Quarterfinal win. Their blend of defensive solidity and explosive attacking talent makes them a formidable contender for the World Cup title.

Spain's Resilience Proves Key to Advancement

Spain demonstrated remarkable resilience, overcoming a tough Belgian challenge with a late winner. Their ability to maintain composure and find crucial goals under pressure highlights their championship pedigree.

Injury Update: Courtois Out for Belgium

Belgium's star goalkeeper, Thibaut Courtois, suffered an injury during the match against Spain and was replaced by Senne Lammens. While a significant blow for Belgium, it does not impact the advancing teams.

Historical Paths to the Semifinals

France, a 2018 champion and 2022 finalist, continues its deep tournament runs, reaching an eighth World Cup semifinal. Spain, champions in 2010, makes its second-ever appearance in the World Cup semifinals, showcasing their consistent elite-level performance.

Semifinal Preview: France vs Spain

European Giants Collide in Dallas

The first semifinal will feature a monumental clash between two European powerhouses, France and Spain. This highly anticipated encounter is scheduled for July 14 at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas.

Tactical Battle: French Pace vs Spanish Possession

This semifinal promises a fascinating tactical battle, pitting France's blistering pace and direct attacking style against Spain's renowned possession-based football and intricate passing. The midfield duel will be crucial in dictating the tempo of the game.

Head-to-Head History: A Tightly Contested Rivalry

Historically, matches between France and Spain have been tightly contested affairs, with both teams enjoying periods of dominance. Recent encounters have often been decided by fine margins, setting the stage for another thrilling chapter in their rivalry.
