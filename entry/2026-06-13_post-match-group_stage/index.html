Match Summary

Qatar secured a historic first World Cup point by forcing a dramatic 1-1 draw against Switzerland at Levi's Stadium, with late captain Boualem Khoukhi erasing Breel Embolo's first-half penalty. Brazil and Morocco also played to a 1-1 stalemate at MetLife Stadium, with Vinícius Júnior's clinical finish canceling out Ismael Saibari's early strike, leaving Group C's favorites without a winning start.
