⚽Quarter-Finals Recap

Spain Edges Belgium in Thrilling Quarter-Final

Spain secured a hard-fought 2-1 victory over Belgium in a captivating Quarter-Final clash. The match, held at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, saw Spain advance to the semi-finals.

Brazil Overcomes Netherlands in Tense Encounter

Brazil advanced to the semi-finals after defeating the Netherlands 2-1 in a tightly contested Quarter-Final. The South American giants showcased their resilience at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, setting up a highly anticipated next round.

🗓️Semi-Finals Outlook

Spain to Face Brazil in Blockbuster Semi-Final

The first semi-final will feature a clash of titans as Spain takes on Brazil. Both teams have displayed exceptional form throughout the tournament, promising an intense battle for a spot in the final at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta, Georgia.

France and England Set for European Showdown

France and England are set to reignite their rivalry in the second semi-final, following their respective Quarter-Final victories. This highly anticipated European derby will take place at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, with a place in the World Cup final at stake.

✨Player Spotlight & Injuries

Richarlison's Form Propels Brazil

Brazil's Richarlison has been in scintillating form, consistently delivering crucial goals and high-energy performances. His clinical finishing and relentless work rate have been key factors in Brazil's progression to the semi-finals.

Mbappé Sustains Minor Knock

Reports indicate that France's star forward Kylian Mbappé sustained a minor knock during their Quarter-Final match. While not considered serious, his fitness will be closely monitored ahead of the crucial semi-final clash against England.

🧠Tactical Insights

Spain's tactical approach has emphasized defensive solidity, allowing them to control games and launch effective counter-attacks. Their disciplined backline and midfield pressing have proven difficult for opponents to break down.
