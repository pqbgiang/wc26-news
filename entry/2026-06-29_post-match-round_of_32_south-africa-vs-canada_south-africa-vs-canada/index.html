Match Results

South Africa 0-1 Canada

Canada dominated possession and created the majority of the chances, but struggled to break down a well-organized South African defense for much of the match. South Africa adopted a cautious approach, aiming to absorb pressure and hit on the counter, but ultimately lacked the attacking quality to trouble Canada's goal.

Analysis

Match Summary

Canada secured a historic first-ever World Cup knockout stage victory with a dramatic stoppage-time goal from Stephen Eustaquio, sending them into the Quarterfinals. South Africa, despite a valiant defensive effort and reaching the knockout phase for the first time, was unable to find an equalizer and exits the tournament.
