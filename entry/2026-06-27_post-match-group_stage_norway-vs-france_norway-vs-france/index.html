Match Results

Norway 1-4 France

France's 4-2-3-1 formation effectively exploited Norway's defensive vulnerabilities, especially in transition, leading to a dominant first-half performance. Norway, playing a 4-3-3 with a heavily rotated squad, struggled to contain France's attacking rotations and lacked a consistent threat despite pulling one goal back.

Analysis

Match Summary

France secured a dominant 4-1 victory over Norway, with Ousmane Dembélé's first-half hat-trick sealing their perfect record and top spot in Group I. Despite Norway's efforts, including a penalty save by Mike Maignan, France's attacking prowess proved too much, setting them up as strong contenders for the knockout stages.
