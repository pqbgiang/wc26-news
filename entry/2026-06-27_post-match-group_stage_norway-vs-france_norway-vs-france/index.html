Match Results

Norway 1-4 France

France dominated the match early on, utilizing Kylian Mbappé's playmaking and Ousmane Dembélé's clinical finishing to exploit a heavily rotated Norwegian defense. Despite Norway's brief resurgence after Aasgaard's goal, France maintained control in the second half, sealing the victory with a late stoppage-time header from Désiré Doué.

Match Summary

Ousmane Dembélé's spectacular first-half hat-trick propelled France to a dominant 4-1 victory over a heavily rotated Norway side, securing top spot in Group I with a perfect group-stage record. Despite resting Erling Haaland, Norway advanced to the Round of 32 in second place, where they will face Ivory Coast.
