Match Results

Canada 0-3 Morocco

Morocco displayed a clinical performance, capitalizing on their chances with a patient approach and effective counter-attacks. Canada, despite their high-intensity play and efforts to create opportunities, struggled to convert against a resolute Moroccan defense.

Morocco delivered a commanding performance to overcome co-hosts Canada, showcasing their clinical finishing and defensive solidity in a highly anticipated Round of 16 clash.
