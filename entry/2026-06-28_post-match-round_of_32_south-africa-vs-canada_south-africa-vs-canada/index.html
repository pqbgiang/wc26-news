Stephen Eustaquio's Masterclass: South Africa Dominates Canada

▸ KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • •South Africa 0-1 Canada
  • •South Africa goalkeeper Ronwen Williams set a new World Cup record for a keeper with 85 successful passes.
  • •Stephen Eustaquio (Canada): Scored the match-winning goal and was a key creative force, creating 5+ chances from set-pieces.

Match Results

South Africa 0-1 Canada

Canada, employing a 4-4-2 system, focused on direct play and dynamic attacking transitions, which eventually broke down South Africa's resilient defense. South Africa, in a 4-2-3-1, prioritized defensive solidity and sought counter-attacking opportunities, with their goalkeeper playing a notable role in ball distribution.
