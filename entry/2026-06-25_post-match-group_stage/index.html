Analysis

Match Summary

South Africa secured a historic 1-0 victory over South Korea, with Thapelo Maseko's second-half goal sending them into the World Cup knockout stages for the first time ever. This result means South Africa finishes second in Group A, while South Korea now faces an anxious wait to see if their three points and -1 goal difference are enough to qualify as one of the best third-placed teams.
