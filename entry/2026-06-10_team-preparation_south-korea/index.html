🏥Squad Status & Injuries

Cho Yu-min Ruled Out

South Korea defender Cho Yu-min is ruled out with a foot injury. This forces Hong Myung-bo to adjust defensive pairing options ahead of Group A matches.

26-Man Squad Finalized

South Korea announced their final squad on May 16. All players cleared for tournament competition with Cho Yu-min as only late withdrawal due to injury sustained in Utah training.

👥Key Squad Composition

Goalkeeping: Kim Seung-gyu Leads

Veteran Seung-gyu Kim is expected to be first-choice keeper at the World Cup, with over 80 international appearances and previous experience in international tournaments. Hyeon-woo Jo (Ulsan HD) provides reliable backup.
Key Players:
  • Seung-gyu Kim
  • Hyeon-woo Jo

Defense: Min-jae Kim Anchors

Yu-min Cho of Sharjah is another reliable centre-back and should form a solid partnership with Min-jae as South Korea's central defensive duo at the World Cup. However, with Cho now injured, alternatives emerge.

Midfield & Attack Firepower

Feyenoord's Hwang In-beom provides central stability; Wolves forward Hwang Hee-chan and PSG's Lee Kang-in inject creative flair, while Birmingham City's Paik Seung-ho, Stoke City's Bae Jun-ho, and Celtic's Yang Hyun-jun offer incredible dynamism.
Key Players:
  • Son Heung-min
  • Hwang In-beom
  • Lee Kang-in
  • Hwang Hee-chan

⚡Form & Morale

Unbeaten Qualifying Campaign

The only Asian side to go unbeaten in qualifying, the Republic of Korea will arrive with momentum. This qualification success builds confidence ahead of Group A fixtures.

Son Heung-min Sets Tone

Described as 'the heart' of the team by coach Hong Myung-Bo, Son has called on his side not to show stronger teams more respect, urging belief as the Koreans look to make a statement. Team mentality emphasises aggressive, confident approach.

🎯Tactical Setup

Hong Myung-bo's Philosophy

Led by star winger Son Heungmin, the Republic of Korea head into the FIFA World Cup 2026, aiming to embrace a more aggressive approach. Hong Myung-Bo describes Son as 'the heart' of the team. Shift toward attacking football with possession control.
Formation:
4-2-3-1

Midfield Control Priority

The engine room will see Feyenoord's maestro Hwang In-beom partner Birmingham City's Paik Seung-ho, with the duo playing pivotal roles in maintaining structural stability. Emphasis on tempo control and defensive security.

🏆Group A Opponents & Fixtures

Czechia Opener - June 11, 2026

Thursday, June 11: South Korea vs. Czechia, 10 p.m. at Guadalajara Stadium. First group match follows Mexico vs South Africa curtain-raiser. Czechia presents organised, European defensive challenge.

Mexico Clash - June 18, 2026

Thursday, June 18: Mexico vs. South Korea, 9 p.m. Critical group match against co-host Mexico. Mexico favoured but South Korea possesses attacking talent to trouble them.

South Africa Finale - June 24, 2026

Wednesday, June 24: South Africa vs. South Korea, 9 p.m. at Monterrey Stadium. Final group match; South Korea must manage matchday 3 fixture complexity. Head-to-head history: 1-1 draw (2010 World Cup opener).

📊Group Stage Outlook

South Korea as Group Contender

South Korea is the second pick in Group A predictions. Son Heung-min gives them a match-winner in any game. Realistic path to knockout qualification alongside Mexico.

Tournament Narrative

After being knocked out in the group stages during their 2014 and 2018 World Cup campaigns, they managed to break that streak by qualifying for the Round of 16 in 2022. However, their run was halted by Brazil in the knockout stage. 2026 presents opportunity for deeper run.
