Match Results

Panama 0-2 England

England dominated possession but struggled to penetrate Panama's deep-lying 5-4-1 defense in a goalless first half. The Three Lions found their breakthrough in the second half through a set-piece and a quick follow-up goal, which effectively sealed their victory.

Match Summary

England secured their spot atop Group L with a decisive 2-0 victory over Panama, overcoming a resilient defensive effort from the Central American side. Jude Bellingham and Harry Kane delivered the crucial second-half goals, ensuring England's progression to the Round of 32.
