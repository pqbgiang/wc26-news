Match Results

Brazil 2-1 Japan

Japan executed a disciplined defensive strategy after taking an early lead through a counter-attack, aiming to frustrate Brazil. Brazil dominated possession and relentlessly attacked, eventually breaking down Japan's resistance with two second-half goals to secure the comeback victory.
