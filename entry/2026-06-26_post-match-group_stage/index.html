Analysis

Match Summary

Ivory Coast secured a historic qualification to the World Cup knockout stages for the first time, defeating debutants Curaçao 2-0 with a brace from Nicolas Pepe. This victory cemented their second-place finish in Group E, while Curaçao's inaugural World Cup journey concluded without a win.
