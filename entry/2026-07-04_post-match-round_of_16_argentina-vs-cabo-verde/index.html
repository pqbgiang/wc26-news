Match Results

Argentina 3-2 (AET) Cabo Verde

Argentina, despite dominating possession, struggled to break down a resilient Cabo Verde defense that played with discipline and courage. Cabo Verde effectively countered, twice equalizing and pushing the reigning champions to their limits in an extra-time thriller.
3-2 (AET)
📋 Timeline
⚽
Lionel Messi opened the scoring in the 29th minute, extending his World Cup goal record.
📌
Deroy Duarte equalized for Cabo Verde in the 58th minute, stunning the defending champions and forcing extra time.
📌
Sidny Lopes Cabral scored a spectacular equalizer in the 103rd minute of extra time, making it 2-2.
⚽
An own goal by Diney Borges in the 111th minute, deflecting a Cristian Romero header, ultimately secured Argentina's victory.
⚽
Cabo Verde's goalkeeper Vozinha made 10 crucial saves, including five against Messi, to keep his team in contention.
