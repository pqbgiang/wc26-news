Quarterfinal Thrillers Deliver Semifinalists

Brazil Edges Germany in Tactical Masterclass

Brazil secured a hard-fought 2-1 victory over Germany in a captivating Quarterfinal clash. Vinicius Jr. opened the scoring in the 28th minute with a clinical finish, assisted by Rodrygo, showcasing Brazil's fluid attack. Germany equalized through Jamal Musiala in the 55th minute, but a late header from Marquinhos in the 88th minute, off a corner, sealed Brazil's path to the semifinals.

Tite's Tactical Brilliance Outwits Nagelsmann

Brazil's tactical setup under Tite proved decisive, with their high press disrupting Germany's midfield rhythm and their wingers exploiting wide channels. Nagelsmann's Germany struggled to break down Brazil's disciplined defense, despite moments of individual brilliance from Musiala. The late set-piece goal highlighted Brazil's resilience and strategic depth.

Vinicius Jr. and Marquinhos Shine for Seleção

Vinicius Jr.'s opening goal and constant threat down the left flank earned him Man of the Match honors, demonstrating his elite-level acceleration and finishing. Marquinhos's late winner underscored his leadership and aerial prowess, proving crucial in a tight contest. Rodrygo's assist further solidified his role as a key creative force.

Spain Overcomes Italy in Extra Time Battle

Spain advanced to the semifinals after a grueling 1-0 extra-time victory over Italy in a fiercely contested Quarterfinal. The match remained goalless through 90 minutes, a testament to both teams' defensive solidity and tactical discipline. Pedri scored the decisive goal in the 108th minute, breaking the deadlock with a sublime finish from outside the box.

De La Fuente's Possession Dominance Pays Off

Spain's relentless possession-based football eventually wore down Italy's resolute defense, with Luis de la Fuente's side controlling the tempo throughout. Luciano Spalletti's Italy defended admirably, relying on counter-attacks, but ultimately succumbed to Spain's sustained pressure in extra time. The midfield battle was a chess match, with Spain's technical superiority eventually prevailing.

Pedri's Moment of Magic Secures Spanish Triumph

Pedri was the undisputed standout performer, dictating play from midfield and capping his exceptional display with the winning goal. His vision, passing range, and composure under pressure were instrumental in Spain's progression. Rodri also delivered a commanding performance, shielding the defense and initiating attacks.
