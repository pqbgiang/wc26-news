⚔️Semi-Final Showdown

France vs. Spain Set for Epic Semi-Final

France will face Spain in a highly anticipated World Cup 2026 semi-final on July 14. The match, kicking off at 19:00 UTC, will be held at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas. Both teams have shown formidable form throughout the tournament, setting the stage for a thrilling encounter.

⚽Quarter-Final Highlights

Spain Edges Belgium in Quarter-Final Thriller

Spain secured their semi-final spot by defeating Belgium 2-1 in a hard-fought quarter-final match at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles. Fabián Ruiz scored in the first half, with Mikel Merino sealing the victory in the 87th minute.

France Dominates Morocco to Advance

France booked their place in the semi-finals with a convincing 2-0 victory over Morocco in their quarter-final clash. Goals from Kylian Mbappé in the 60th minute and Ousmane Dembélé six minutes later ensured France's progression.

⭐Key Player Performances

Mbappé Leads Golden Boot Race

Kylian Mbappé continues his sensational form, leading the tournament's scoring charts with eight goals. His consistent performance has been crucial to France's dominant run to the semi-finals.

Merino's Clutch Performances for Spain

Mikel Merino has emerged as a pivotal player for Spain in the knockout stages, delivering crucial goals against both Portugal and Belgium. His timely contributions have been instrumental in Spain's progression.

📈Dominant Campaigns

France's Unstoppable Offensive Power

France enters the semi-finals as the highest-scoring team of the tournament, demonstrating exceptional attacking prowess. They dominated their group with a perfect nine points and a +8 goal difference, continuing their strong form into the knockout rounds.

Spain's Impregnable Defense

Spain has showcased an impressive defensive record, topping their group with seven points and conceding zero goals. Their ability to shut down opponents has been a cornerstone of their successful campaign.
