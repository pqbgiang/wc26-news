Quarterfinal Thrillers: European Giants Advance

Bellingham's Brace Sends England Past Norway in Extra Time

England secured a hard-fought 2-1 extra-time victory over Norway, with Jude Bellingham scoring both goals for the Three Lions. Erling Haaland netted for Norway, but his efforts were not enough to prevent England from advancing to the semifinals. The match was a tense affair, pushing both teams to their limits.

Three Lions' Resilience Shines Against Haaland's Threat

England's tactical setup focused on containing Erling Haaland, a strategy that ultimately paid off in extra time. The midfield battle, particularly between Declan Rice and Martin Odegaard, was pivotal in controlling the tempo and creating opportunities. England's defensive solidity, combined with Bellingham's attacking prowess, proved decisive.

Bellingham and Haaland: A Duel of Golden Boot Contenders

Jude Bellingham's two goals were a testament to his elite-level performance, showcasing his ability to deliver in critical moments. Erling Haaland, despite his goal, was largely marshaled by the English defense, highlighting the challenge of breaking down top-tier opposition. Both players remain key figures in the Golden Boot race.

Messi Magic: Mac Allister Header Secures Argentina's Semifinal Berth

Argentina edged past a resilient Switzerland side with a narrow 1-0 victory, thanks to an early header from Alexis Mac Allister. The decisive goal came in the 10th minute from a perfectly delivered corner kick by captain Lionel Messi. Switzerland's disciplined defense made it a challenging encounter for the reigning champions.

Switzerland's organized defensive structure proved difficult to break down, forcing Argentina to rely on a set-piece moment of brilliance. Mac Allister's Decisive Strike from Messi's Corner

Alexis Mac Allister's early goal, assisted by Lionel Messi, was the singular moment of breakthrough in a tightly contested match. This combination highlights the importance of set-pieces and individual brilliance at this stage of the tournament. Messi continues to be a central figure in Argentina's attacking plays.

Road to Glory: Semifinalists' Form and Fitness

England and Argentina Punch Tickets to the Elite Four

England showcased their fighting spirit by overcoming Norway in extra time, demonstrating strong form and mental fortitude. Argentina, while not dominant, found a way to win against a tough Swiss side, relying on their star power. Both teams enter the semifinals with momentum, albeit through different paths.

Injury Concerns Loom for Semifinal Contenders

France faces concerns over Kylian Mbappé's minor ankle injury, though he is expected to play, alongside doubts for Kouadio Kone and Aurélien Tchouaméni. Spain has Nico Williams and Yeremy Pino doubtful. England's Declan Rice, Marc Guehi, and Reece James are reportedly fit after recent issues, while Argentina monitors Cristian Romero's knee.

Historical Paths Converge for Semifinal Showdowns

England's victory marks their fourth-ever World Cup semifinal appearance, showcasing their consistent presence at the tournament's sharp end. Argentina, as reigning champions, continue their quest to become the first back-to-back winners since Brazil in 1962, adding another chapter to their storied World Cup history.

France vs. Spain: A European Derby in the Semifinals

Recent History Favors La Roja in France-Spain Rivalry

Spain holds a recent advantage over France, having defeated them in the semifinals of both Euro 2024 and the 2025 Nations League. This history sets the stage for a compelling tactical battle, with both teams eager to assert dominance on the biggest stage.

Mbappé's Firepower Meets Spain's Control and Super-Subs

France's potent attack, spearheaded by Kylian Mbappé, will challenge Spain's renowned possession-based football and their ability to introduce game-changing players like Mikel Merino and Lamine Yamal from the bench. The midfield battle will be crucial in determining control and creating scoring opportunities.

Decades-Long Feud Ignites Again

England and Argentina share a decades-long rivalry in World Cup history, marked by memorable and often controversial encounters. This semifinal adds another chapter to their intense footballing narrative, promising a passionate contest.

Kane and Bellingham vs. Messi's Enduring Genius
