France and Morocco Clash in High Stakes Quarterfinal

France advanced by dominating their Round of 16 fixture with clinical finishing, while Morocco secured their spot through a resilient defensive display and tactical discipline. Both teams have shown consistency, but France must overcome Morocco's compact low block to avoid a potential upset. Kylian Mbappe remains the focal point for France, facing immense pressure to break down a Moroccan side that has conceded very few goals this tournament.
