Is Mexico Match-Ready?

▸ KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • •Goalkeeper Malagón Out with ACL Tear

  • •Lozano & Huescas Excluded Due to Fitness

👥Squad & Injury Status

Goalkeeper Malagón Out with ACL Tear

Luis Ángel Malagón, expected to start in net for Mexico, suffered a torn ACL early in the year and will miss the tournament. This saw Guillermo Ochoa back into the national team fold after the program had seemingly moved on from the 40-year-old legend.

Ochoa Confirmed for Record Sixth World Cup

Evergreen goalkeeper Guillermo Ochoa could feature in his sixth World Cup campaign. Ochoa, age 40, is set to take part in a record sixth World Cup, level with Cristiano Ronaldo.

Lozano & Huescas Excluded Due to Fitness

Hirving Lozano was left out because of disciplinary issues and limited playing time with San Diego FC. Rodrigo Huescas was excluded as he continues recovering from a serious knee injury.

Returning Players Being Monitored

Aguirre decided to monitor several players returning from injuries, including Luis Chávez, César Huerta, Julián Araujo, and captain Edson Álvarez, who is expected to be one of the emotional and tactical leaders of the squad.

⚡Current Form & Morale

Strong Pre-Tournament Run

Mexico have been in strong form recently, winning 3 of their last 5 matches (60% win rate), scoring 9 goals and conceding 2 during this period.

Recent Competitive Success

Under Javier Aguirre's third spell, Mexico led them to victory in the 2024/25 CONCACAF Nations League and then led Mexico to the 2025 CONCACAF Gold Cup crown, beating the U.S. in the final.

Home Advantage Expectations

After quarter-final finishes at the home editions in 1970 and 1986, Mexico arrive at this year's global football showpiece, hoping to replicate the success of their 2025 CONCACAF Gold Cup campaign.

🏆Group Stage Fixtures

Opening Match vs South Africa

Thursday, June 11: Group A: Mexico vs. South Africa (Mexico City), 1 p.m. local / 3 p.m. ET. The two teams have faced each other four times, most recently in a 1–1 draw in the 2010 FIFA World Cup opening match, with South Africa as the host nation. This match will take place exactly 16 years after.

Second Match vs South Korea

Mexico then face South Korea on June 18 in Guadalajara. The teams have played three times, most recently in a 2016 friendly, a 2–1 victory for the South Koreans.

Final Group Match vs Czechia

Mexico face Czechia on June 24 in Mexico City. Mexico and Czechia have met 15 times including twice in the World Cup, with Mexico winning both matches: 3–1 in the 1998 tournament in France, and 2–1 in the 2018 edition in Russia.

⚙️Key Players & Tactical Setup

Veteran Striker Jiménez in Prime Form

All eyes will be on Mexico's hero and veteran striker, Raúl Jiménez. The 34-year-old arrives at his home World Cup after a strong season with Premier League club Fulham, in which he reached double digits in the scoring charts.

Captain Edson Álvarez as Tactical Leader

Experienced midfielder Edson Alvarez has been included on the roster, having only just barely returned from ankle surgery before the end of the club season. He anchors the midfield and leadership structure.

Midfield Depth & Experience

Mexico have abundance of talent in young midfielders Gilberto Mora and Obed Vargas. With five players in the squad who have at least 90 international appearances and nine more with at least 40, Mexico relies heavily on experience.

🎯Tournament Outlook

Pressure of Home Hosting

Mexico is preparing for one of the biggest moments in its football history. The 2026 FIFA World Cup will be hosted by Mexico, the United States and Canada, and El Tri hopes home-field advantage can help deliver a historic run. Mexico will once again try to use the energy of its fans to reach at least the quarterfinals, a stage the national team has only achieved when hosting the World Cup in 1970 and 1986.

Betting Odds & Realistic Ceiling

Mexico are long shots to win the tournament despite home advantage, sitting well down the outright board behind the European and South American contenders. Prices range from around 67.00 to 81.00 in decimal, a sign of how differently sportsbooks weigh a home run against the squad's ceiling.

Group A Scenario

The group consists of Mexico (co-host), South Africa, South Korea, and Czechia. Mexico has not reached a World Cup quarterfinal since 1986 when current manager Javier Aguirre was playing on the team.
