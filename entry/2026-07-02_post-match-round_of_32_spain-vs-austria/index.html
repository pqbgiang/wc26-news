Match Results

Spain 3-0 Austria

Spain dominated possession and consistently pressed Austria's deep defensive line, creating numerous scoring opportunities throughout the match. Austria attempted to absorb pressure and counter-attack, but struggled to break down Spain's organized defense and generate significant chances.

Analysis

Match Summary

Spain delivered a dominant performance in their Round of 32 clash, comfortably defeating Austria to secure their spot in the Quarterfinals. Their attacking prowess and solid defense proved too much for the Austrian side, ending their World Cup journey.
