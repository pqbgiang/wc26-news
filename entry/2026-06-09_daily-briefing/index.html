⚽Tournament Kickoff

Mexico vs South Africa opens 2026 World Cup

Mexico hosts South Africa in the opening match at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City on June 11. The tournament runs from June 11 to July 19, 2026, marking the first World Cup to expand to 48 teams across three host nations.

48-team format brings historic expansion

The tournament will be the first FIFA World Cup to be hosted by three nations and the first to include 48 teams, an expansion from 32 previously. 32 teams will advance to the knockout round following the group stage; the top two teams from each group will move on along with the eight best third-place teams.

🏟️Host Nations Status

USA begins Group D campaign Friday

The United States will begin its tournament June 12 at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California against Paraguay. The Americans will then play Australia before finishing the group stage against Türkiye.

Canada faces Bosnia in Toronto

Canada is in Group B and will open against Bosnia and Herzegovina on June 12 at BMO Field in Toronto. Canada will compete alongside Switzerland, Qatar, and Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Mexico anchors Group A as regional powerhouse

Mexico is in Group A alongside South Africa, Korea Republic, and Czechia. As a host nation, Mexico opens the tournament in Mexico City and plays group-stage matches in Guadalajara and Monterrey.

🏥Injury Updates

Netherlands loses Timber to groin injury

Defender Jurriën Timber departed the team's camp due to a lingering groin injury, saying he was not recovered enough to participate in the World Cup in a medically responsible manner. Despite appearing in the Champions League final, he hasn't shown enough progress, leading the Dutch team to replace him with Lutsharel Geertruida.

Germany ruled out 18-year-old Karl

Germany will be without one of its most promising players, as Karl has been ruled out for the World Cup. The 18-year-old, who had 17 goal contributions for Bayern Munich this season, could miss up to two months.

USA's Richards expected ready vs Paraguay

Richards didn't face Germany in the United States' final World Cup tuneup, but the Crystal Palace defender recently said he'll be ready for Friday's opener against Paraguay. The U.S. men's national team enters the tournament after a disappointing 2-1 loss to Germany in Chicago, a result that did little to ease concerns about Mauricio Pochettino's squad, with center back Chris Richards having yet to return from an ankle injury.

Argentina's Messi targets opener despite hamstring

Messi left his most recent Inter Miami appearance early with a hamstring issue, though it was seemingly a precautionary move. The eight-time Ballon d'Or winner didn't play in Argentina's latest pre-World Cup friendly but is likely to be available for its opener against Algeria.

Brazil's Estêvão ruled out with hamstring

Estêvão pulled up against Manchester United in April with a hamstring injury that left him in tears. The initial prognosis was positive, suggesting a two-week absence, but it soon became clear things were much more serious, and he has been left off Brazil's preliminary squad.

🌍Global Representation

Africa sends record 10 teams

Africa sends ten teams to the 2026 World Cup, its largest ever representation. Morocco leads the group after its historic 2022 semi final run. The African qualifiers include Algeria, Cape Verde, Egypt, Ghana, Ivory Coast, Morocco, Senegal, South Africa, Tunisia.

Europe sends 16 teams to tournament

Europe sends 16 teams to the 2026 World Cup. France, Spain, England, Portugal and Germany enter as major contenders, while Bosnia and Herzegovina returns after its 2014 debut.

South America maintains six-team presence

South America sends six teams to the 2026 World Cup. Argentina arrives as defending champion, while Brazil keeps its record of appearing at every men's World Cup.

📊Tournament Structure

New pathway prevents top teams early meetings

In the pursuit of competitive balance, two pathways have been created to the semifinals that will prevent the two highest-ranking teams from meeting before the final should they win their respective groups. Spain is the highest-ranked team in the tournament, with defending champion Argentina ranked second. Those two nations were drawn into opposite pathways.

Round of 32 adds elimination stage depth

The knockout stage will be played in a single-elimination format, starting with the round of 32 on June 28 and culminating with the final on July 19. The eight best third-place teams will advance to the Round of 32.
