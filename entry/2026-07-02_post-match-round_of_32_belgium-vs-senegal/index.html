Youri Tielemans's Masterclass: Belgium Dominates Senegal

▸ KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • •Belgium 3-2 AET Senegal
  • •Habib Diarra opened the scoring for Senegal in the 25th minute, capitalizing on a rebound after Ismaïla Sarr's header hit the post.
  • •Youri Tielemans (Belgium): Scored the equalizer and the winning penalty in extra time.

Match Results

Belgium 3-2 AET Senegal

Senegal initially executed a strong game plan, utilizing pace on the wings and effective counter-attacks to build a two-goal lead. Belgium, after tactical substitutions, shifted to a more aggressive pressing style, overwhelming Senegal in the final minutes of regulation and extra time.
3-2 AET
📋 Timeline

Habib Diarra opened the scoring for Senegal in the 25th minute, capitalizing on a rebound after Ismaïla Sarr's header hit the post.

Ismaïla Sarr doubled Senegal's lead in the 51st minute with a brilliant chest control and finish from a long pass.
📌

Youri Tielemans equalized for Belgium in the 89th minute, forcing the match into extra time.

Analysis

Match Summary

Belgium staged an extraordinary comeback in the Round of 32, overturning a two-goal deficit against Senegal with two late goals in regulation time. The drama culminated in extra time when Youri Tielemans converted a penalty in the 125th minute, securing a thrilling 3-2 victory and breaking Senegalese hearts.
