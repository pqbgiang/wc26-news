⚽R32 Match Recaps (July 2)

Brazil Overcomes Japan in R32 Thriller

Brazil secured a 2-1 victory over Japan in a hard-fought Round of 32 match. Japan's early lead was overturned by a strong second-half performance from the South American giants. This result sees Brazil advance to the Round of 16.

Germany Falls to Paraguay on Penalties

Paraguay caused a major upset, defeating Germany 4-3 on penalties after a 1-1 draw in their Round of 32 encounter. Manuel Neuer saved an early shot, but Paraguay's resilience ultimately paid off, sending them through to the next round.

Morocco Advances Past Netherlands on Penalties

Morocco emerged victorious against the Netherlands, winning 3-2 in a penalty shootout after their Round of 32 match ended 1-1. This dramatic win highlights Morocco's strong defensive organization and composure under pressure.
