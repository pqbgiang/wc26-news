⚽️Round of 16 Highlights

Brazil Edges Uruguay in Thriller

Brazil secured a hard-fought 2-1 victory over Uruguay in a captivating Round of 16 clash. Vinicius Jr. netted the decisive goal in the 82nd minute, sending the five-time champions into the quarter-finals.

France Dominates Poland 3-0

Kylian Mbappé delivered a stellar performance, scoring two goals as France comfortably defeated Poland 3-0. The reigning champions showcased their attacking prowess, securing their spot in the next round with a dominant display.

🏆Quarter-Finalists Update

Early Quarter-Finalists Emerge

With two Round of 16 matches concluded, Brazil and France are the first teams to officially book their places in the quarter-finals. Both teams demonstrated strong form and tactical discipline in their respective victories.
