⚽Yesterday's Round of 32 Results

Spain Dominates Austria in Knockout Opener

Spain secured a comfortable 3-0 victory over Austria in their Round of 32 clash. The European champions are looking to add a second World Cup title to their history.

Portugal Edges Croatia in Thriller

Portugal narrowly defeated Croatia in a dramatic 2-1 comeback, with Cristiano Ronaldo scoring a penalty. Croatia had an equalizer disallowed by VAR in the final moments, leading to fan protests.

Switzerland Advances Past Algeria

Switzerland secured a 2-1 victory against Algeria to progress to the next stage of the tournament. Goals from Ruben Vargas and Johan Manzambi sealed the win for the Swiss.

🗓️Today's Knockout Schedule

Australia Faces Egypt in Arlington

Australia will take on Egypt in a highly anticipated Round of 32 match. Egypt advanced as runners-up from Group G and will look to continue their run.

Argentina Meets Cape Verde in Miami

Group J winners Argentina will face Cape Verde, who progressed as Group H runners-up in their World Cup debut. This match is set to be a challenging encounter for both sides.

Colombia Takes on Ghana in Kansas City

Colombia and Ghana will battle it out in Kansas City for a spot in the next round. Ghana advanced as one of the best third-placed teams from Group L.

🩹Injury Updates

Ismael Kone Out for Canada

Canadian midfielder Ismael Kone suffered a fractured leg and underwent emergency surgery, ruling him out for the remainder of the tournament. His absence is a significant blow to Canada's squad.

Schlotterbeck Sidelined for Germany

Germany's Niklas Süle (reported as Nikico Schlottterbeck in source) sustained a rupture of the medial ligament in his left ankle during the match against Ivory Coast. The defender will miss the rest of the World Cup.

Raphinha Doubtful for Brazil's Later Stages

Brazil's Raphinha suffered a muscle injury in the back of his right thigh during the match against Haiti. While there's hope for a return in the final stages, his participation in upcoming matches is highly unlikely.

Neymar's Calf Strain Progressing

Neymar continues to recover from a calf injury, with recent medical tests showing encouraging progress. Optimism is growing for his availability early in the tournament, though he has yet to return to full training.
