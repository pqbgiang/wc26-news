⚽Tournament Kickoff

Mexico vs South Africa opener

Co-host Mexico plays South Africa in the opening match at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City at 3 p.m. ET. South Korea and Czechia follow in Group A later today. The 48-team tournament spanning 39 days begins with opening ceremonies across the three host nations.

Tournament format confirmed

The expanded format features 12 groups of four teams with top two plus eight best third-place teams advancing. Round of 32 knockout stage begins June 28, with the final at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey on July 19.

🏥Injury Updates

Argentina: Messi returning from hamstring

Lionel Messi played final 20 minutes against Iceland and converted a penalty after pre-tournament hamstring concern. Argentina opens against Algeria on June 16. Paredes, Molina, Paz, and Montiel training separately as precaution but available.

Brazil: Multiple attacking absences

Rodrygo (torn ACL/meniscus) and Estêvão (hamstring) ruled out. Neymar (calf) showed MRI progress but unlikely for Morocco opener on June 19. Ancelotti's squad faces significant attacking depth challenges early.

Spain: Yamal expected for Cape Verde

Lamine Yamal (groin/hamstring) cleared to play against Cape Verde on June 15 after no action since April 22. Coach de la Fuente indicated possible minutes restrictions for Spain's opener.

Canada: Davies returns to training

Alphonso Davies recovered from hamstring injury and returned to full training. Canada remains cautious with its captain ahead of Bosnia and Herzegovina opener, but his availability is trending positively.

USA: Richards confirmed fit

Center back Chris Richards, who missed friendlies with ankle injury, confirmed readiness to play. Patrick Agyemang (Achilles) and Johnny Cardoso (ankle) ruled out for USMNT. USA face Paraguay on June 12.

Netherlands: De Ligt out, Timber exits

Matthijs de Ligt (back) underwent surgery and unavailable. Jurriën Timber ruled out due to injury. Major defensive concerns for Oranje ahead of group stage.

Mexico: Malagon out, Ochoa returns

First-choice goalkeeper Luis Ángel Malagon ruptured Achilles tendon. Veteran Guillermo Ochoa, 41, returns for his sixth World Cup to fill the gap following Gold Cup winner absence.

Japan: Mitoma and Minamino absent

Kaoru Mitoma (hamstring) and Takumi Minamino (torn ACL) ruled out, weakening attacking depth. Japan still considered dark horse but without two key forwards.

📊Pre-Tournament Status

Top-ranked teams confirmed

Argentina (No. 1) defend title as favorites. Spain ranked No. 2, followed by France (No. 3), England (No. 4), and Portugal (No. 5). Brazil (No. 6) and traditional powerhouses complete favorites despite injury concerns.

USMNT under new leadership

Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of USA with deeper, more talented roster than any in USMNT history. Host nation advantage present but final-16 ceiling likely without tactical excellence from coach.

Group stage schedule

Group stage runs June 11-27 with 72 matches across 12 groups. First-day matches: Mexico vs South Africa (3 p.m. ET), South Korea vs Czechia (10 p.m. ET). Multiple venues across three host nations.
