Match Results

Senegal 5-0 Iraq

Senegal started aggressively, capitalizing on an early goal and Iraq's subsequent red card to control the match. They patiently built attacks in the first half before unleashing a clinical four-goal surge in the second half. Iraq, despite being reduced to ten men, attempted to maintain their passing game but were ultimately undone by individual errors and overwhelmed by Senegal's numerical advantage and attacking prowess.

Analysis

Match Summary

Senegal's emphatic 5-0 victory over Iraq secured their first points of the tournament and significantly boosted their goal difference, keeping their hopes alive for a spot in the Round of 32 as one of the best third-placed teams. Iraq, already eliminated, finished their World Cup campaign without a point, having been hampered by an early red card and individual errors throughout the match.
