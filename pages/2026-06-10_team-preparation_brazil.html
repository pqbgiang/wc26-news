Brazil Squad Pulse

Wednesday, June 10, 2026

🏥 Squad & Injury Status

Wesley OUT — Thigh Injury Rules Out Right Back

Right back Wesley suffered a left thigh muscle injury during Brazil's pre-tournament friendly against Egypt on June 6 in Cleveland, confirmed by the CBF on June 7. He has been replaced by midfielder Éderson Silva in the 26-man squad. FOX Sports reported the injury was significant enough to rule Wesley out of the entire tournament.

Neymar Calf Concern — Status Uncertain

Neymar, 34, did not travel to Cleveland with the squad for the Egypt friendly due to a calf injury, per FOX Sports. ESPN noted he was handed a squad place after Estêvão's hamstring injury, making him now Brazil's primary injury wildcard heading into Group C.

Estêvão & Rodrygo — Major Absentees

Estêvão tore his hamstring against Manchester United in April and was left off the preliminary squad entirely. Rodrygo suffered a torn ACL and meniscus in Real Madrid's 1-0 loss to Getafe in March, ruling him out for the rest of 2026. ESPN described this as "a major issue" for manager Carlo Ancelotti, stripping Brazil of wing depth.

Militão Also Missing from Defence

Éder Militão suffered a hamstring tear requiring surgery, ruling him out of the World Cup. ESPN noted this elevates Bremer (Juventus), Fabrício Bruno (Cruzeiro), and potentially Roger Ibañez into more prominent defensive roles. Marquinhos and Gabriel Magalhães remain the first-choice centre-back pairing.

⚽ Key Players Available

Vinícius Jr — Star Attacker Leads the Line

Vinícius Júnior is Brazil's standout threat on the left wing and remains fully available. FourFourTwo noted he had a slightly below-par club season but is still expected to be a decisive force at international level. Yahoo Sports confirms the front three built around Vinícius, Raphinha, and Matheus Cunha.

Marquinhos & Gabriel Magalhães — Defensive Spine

Marquinhos remains the cornerstone of the backline, having captained PSG to back-to-back Champions League titles in 2024-25 and 2025-26. Arsenal's Gabriel Magalhães partners him in central defence. Yahoo Sports described them as Brazil's most consistent unit heading into the tournament.

Neymar Earns Late Recall at 34

Neymar earned a place in the final 26 after Estêvão's injury, taking his spot per ESPN. FourFourTwo noted his inclusion is "one of the strongest squads at the tournament" news items, though calf fitness remains a concern. His availability for the opening match vs Morocco is not confirmed.

Casemiro & Bruno Guimarães — Midfield Anchor

Yahoo Sports highlighted that the Casemiro–Bruno Guimarães partnership gives Brazil "genuine security in central midfield, something Brazil have not consistently had since their 2002 triumph." Both are fully fit and expected starters. Lucas Paquetá completes the midfield three.

📊 Recent Form & Friendlies

2-1 Win vs Egypt — Pre-Tournament Confidence

Brazil beat Egypt 2-1 on June 6 at Huntington Bank Field, Cleveland, in their final pre-World Cup warm-up. The result was positive but overshadowed by Wesley's injury exit early in the match. Sofascore confirmed the result.

6-2 Rout of Panama — Attack Firing

Brazil hammered Panama 6-2 on May 31 in their first pre-tournament friendly, signalling strong attacking intent under Carlo Ancelotti. Goal.com's fixture data confirms the scoreline, suggesting the front line is in good rhythm ahead of Group C.

Mixed Results: Lost to France, Beat Croatia

In March 2026 friendlies, Brazil lost 2-1 to France and beat Croatia 3-1. The loss to France raised media questions about defensive shape, while the Croatia win showed attacking quality. The contrasting results reflect the squad's inconsistency theme.

🧠 Manager & Tactical Setup

Ancelotti's 4-3-3 — European Structure for Brazil

Carlo Ancelotti, who took over Brazil in May 2025 after leaving Real Madrid, is the first non-Brazilian to coach the Seleção at a World Cup. Yahoo Sports confirmed his preferred 4-3-3, with Alisson in goal, a back four, midfield trio of Casemiro, Bruno Guimarães, and Paquetá, and a front three of Vinícius, Cunha, and Raphinha.

Ancelotti's Real Madrid Connection — Familiar Bonds

FourFourTwo noted that Ancelotti's recent Real Madrid tenure means several Brazilian players — including Vinícius, Militão (injured), and Rodrygo (injured) — are familiar with his methods, aiding cohesion. His club pedigree spans all five major European leagues.

🗓️ Group C Fixture Preview

Match 1: Brazil vs Morocco — June 13, MetLife Stadium

Brazil open Group C against Morocco on June 13 at MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, New Jersey (6:00 p.m. ET). Morocco are a physically organised, AFCON-pedigree side and rank as the stiffest test in the group. Brazil have never lost a World Cup group-stage game against Morocco.

✅ CONFIRMED
Venue: MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, NJ

Match 2: Brazil vs Haiti — June 19, Philadelphia

Brazil face Haiti on June 19 at Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia (9:00 p.m. ET). Brazil beat Haiti 7-1 in the 2016 Copa América Centenario and have never lost to them. A comfortable win is expected to strengthen goal difference.

✅ CONFIRMED
Venue: Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia

Match 3: Scotland vs Brazil — June 24, Miami

The group concludes on June 24 at Hard Rock Stadium, Miami (6:00 p.m. ET). Brazil hold a perfect World Cup record against Scotland: four previous meetings (1974–1998) yielding three Brazilian wins and one draw. Scotland have never beaten Brazil at a World Cup.

✅ CONFIRMED
Venue: Hard Rock Stadium, Miami Gardens, FL

🔮 Group Stage Prediction

Brazil Expected to Top Group C with Relative Ease

Yahoo Sports' preview stated Brazil will "top Group C with few difficulties against Scotland, Morocco, and Haiti." Morocco provide the toughest challenge on Matchday 1, but Brazil's squad depth and Ancelotti's structure make them heavy favourites for 7–9 points. The expanded format means even a third-place finish would likely still advance them.

Semi-Final the Realistic Peak; Title Within Reach

Yahoo Sports assessed a semi-final exit as "the most probable outcome," with Spain or Argentina likely posing a challenge that exposes defensive fragility. However, the piece also acknowledged the squad has "one of their best chances in over two decades" given Ancelotti's structure and Vinícius Júnior's quality. Key risk factor: injuries to Rodrygo, Estêvão, and Militão thin the squad significantly.
