Colombia Squad Pulse

Wednesday, June 10, 2026

👥 Squad & Roster

Captain James Rodríguez leads experience

James Rodríguez, who won the Golden Boot at the 2014 World Cup, is Colombia's captain; the 34-year-old recently switched to Minnesota United in the MLS. Midfielder Richard Ríos is another key player to watch, having impressed for Jose Mourinho's Benfica this season.

Key Players:
  • James Rodríguez (MLS/Minnesota United)
  • Richard Ríos (SL Benfica)
  • Luis Díaz (Bayern Munich)
  • Davinson Sánchez (Galatasaray)

Star forward in career-best form

Luis Díaz arrives in arguably the form of his career, having moved from Liverpool to Bayern Munich last summer and lifted the Bundesliga in his maiden campaign. Díaz leads Colombia with 21 international goals from 72 appearances.

Defensive backbone anchored by Palace duo

Crystal Palace duo Daniel Muñoz and Jefferson Lerma anchor the right flank and midfield respectively. Galatasaray's Davinson Sánchez and Bologna's Jhon Lucumí form a settled centre-back pairing, with Yerry Mina providing experienced cover.

🏥 Injury Status

No major squad injuries reported

Colombia's full 26-man squad completed World Cup preparations without reported fitness concerns. All key players are available for tournament selection, with squad announced in late May ahead of final friendlies.

📈 Recent Form & Morale

Strong pre-tournament momentum secured

Colombia's previous match was against Jordan in International Friendly Games, with the match ending 2-0 in Colombia's favor. Jhon Arias was the star of the match, scoring both goals as Colombia controlled most of the action and closed their preparations with a convincing victory.

Historic unbeaten run set tone for tournament

Under Lorenzo's stewardship Colombia has guided them to wins over Germany, Brazil, Spain and Uruguay, as well as a 28-match unbeaten run. They reached the final of the 2024 Copa America, only losing to Argentina in extra-time.

Coach Lorenzo rebuilds Colombia project

After Reinaldo Rueda's failure to qualify for Qatar, the Colombian Football Federation turned to Néstor Lorenzo as his replacement in 2022, and the 60-year-old responded by immediately steering the national team back on course and securing qualification for the 2026 tournament.

🏆 Qualifying & Group Status

Finished third in CONMEBOL qualifying

Los Cafeteros sealed their place by finishing third in CONMEBOL qualifying with 28 points and edged Uruguay and Brazil on goal difference. Colombia's success was built largely on their attacking prowess, as they scored 28 goals, the second-most among the qualified South American teams, behind only Argentina.

Group K draw includes Portugal challenge

Group K pits Los Cafeteros against tournament debutants Uzbekistan, DR Congo and Portugal, with the closing fixture against Cristiano Ronaldo's Portugal in Miami potentially deciding the group winners.

⚽ Tactical Outlook

Attacking prowess is primary threat

Spearheaded by Bayern Munich star Luis Diaz, Los Cafeteros have enough attacking firepower to trouble any team in the world. Defensively, however, there was room for improvement, with 18 goals conceded, the most among the top-six finishers in qualifying.

James Rodríguez role still critical despite age

James Rodriguez may not be in his prime anymore, but his country will still be relying on him to lead by example and come up with a few more moments of magic in what is likely his last World Cup. Age and experience define his contribution as senior figure.

🎯 Tournament Expectations

Contenders for knockout advancement

Los Cafeteros were one of four teams from CONMEBOL to qualify and will appear at World Cup 2026 with one of the most intriguing squads at the tournament, which has a mix of experience and breakthrough talent. Colombia are positioned as strong group contenders with capability to challenge elite teams.
