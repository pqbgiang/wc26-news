Paraguay's Pre-Match Status

Wednesday, June 10, 2026

👥 Current Squad & Availability

Core Squad Composition

Paraguay named 3 goalkeepers, 8 defenders, 9 midfielders, and 6 forwards. Gustavo Gomez captains the side, which lands in Group D alongside Australia, Turkey, and the United States.

Key Attacking Players

Julio Enciso, the talented young attacking midfielder who joined BlueCo and Strasbourg from Brighton in summer 2025, scored three goals in Paraguay's qualification campaign. Former Newcastle United ace Miguel Almiron will be central to any hopes they have of going deep into the tournament.

Key Players:
  • Julio Enciso
  • Miguel Almiron
  • Antonio Sanabria

Defensive Foundation

Gustavo Gomez, a former AC Milan centre-back with more 88 senior international caps, leads the backline. Sunderland's Omar Alderete is a defender currently playing Premier League football, and will hope to start given his solid season.

🚨 Injury Crisis & Fitness

Enciso Injury Before Opener

Julio Enciso was stretchered off the field in tears on Friday, just one week before the team's World Cup opener against the U.S., exiting with an apparent leg injury in the 25th minute of a 4-0 friendly win against Nicaragua. Coach Gustavo Alfaro remained 'hopeful' that the injury was not muscular; Enciso suffered a tight hamstring from impact and impact on his waist affecting his quadriceps area.

Contingency Options Available

Without Enciso, La Albirroja will lean on the game-changing talent of Gustavo Caballero, whose speed has made him an X-factor; outside of him, the veteran presence of Miguel Almiron could be needed, while standout defensive midfield talent Andrés Cubas may be relied upon to lock things down more than usual.

📊 Recent Form & Morale

Pre-Tournament Friendly Results

Recent results include a 1-2 loss to USA, a 2-1 win against Mexico, a 1-0 win against Greece, a 1-2 loss to Morocco, and a 4-0 win against Nicaragua. Paraguay have been in strong form recently, winning 3 of their last 5 matches (60% win rate), scoring 9 goals and conceding 5 during this period.

Qualifying Confidence & Historical Context

Despite a difficult opening stretch that left them teetering on the margins of automatic qualification, Paraguay found their footing and produced memorable results, defeating both continental giants Brazil and Argentina along the way to confirm their place among the six South American sides heading to North America. Paraguay heads towards North America for the FIFA World Cup, ending a 16-year absence from football's biggest stage.

🎯 Tactical Setup & Preparation

Formation & System

Paraguay usually operate with a 4-4-2 formation. Paraguay arrive at World Cup 2026 as a disciplined dark horse, powered by Miguel Almiron, defensive steel and Gustavo Alfaro's battle-tested, tournament-ready system.

Coach Gustavo Alfaro Profile

Coach is the vastly experienced Argentinian Gustavo Alfaro, who made the shift into international football with Ecuador in 2020 and had a spell in charge of Costa Rica in between, having spent the entirety of his club management career in Argentina. Alfaro was Ecuador's coach at the last World Cup, where they beat hosts Qatar and drew with the Netherlands but ultimately fell short in the group stage.

🏆 Group D Opponents & Outlook

USA Opener - June 12 at SoFi Stadium

Paraguay will head to the U.S. to complete final preparations ahead of the June 12 World Cup opener against the stars and stripes at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles. In the fall, Paraguay fell 2–1 in a friendly against the USMNT, with Enciso impressing in a wide battle against Max Arfsten; in the World Cup opener, that matchup would have likely set him up in a battle with Fulham's Antonee Robinson.

Tournament Expectations

One of the lesser-fancied squads in the tournament might be in line to produce a few more shocks. Paraguay's finest hour arrived at the 2010 edition in South Africa when they eliminated Japan on penalties in the Round of 16 before being edged out 1-0 by eventual champions Spain in the quarter-finals.
