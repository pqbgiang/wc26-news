Morocco Braces for Battle

Wednesday, June 10, 2026

🏥 Injury Concerns & Squad Availability

Ezzalzouli Rules Out — MCL Sprain

Abde Ezzalzouli was substituted during Morocco's friendly against Norway and subsequently diagnosed with an internal knee ligament sprain, leading to expectations he will miss the 2026 World Cup. With a recovery timeline of 3–4 weeks, he is expected to miss the tournament.

Hakimi Thigh Concern Before Brazil Opener

Achraf Hakimi, the Paris Saint-Germain full-back, suffered a thigh injury during the Champions League semi-final against Bayern Munich, raising concerns about his availability for Morocco's World Cup opener against Brazil on June 13. Ouahbi required full medical clearance before players could return.

Multiple Fitness Concerns Pre-Squad Announcement

Injuries complicated selection significantly, with key players including Achraf Hakimi, Chadi Riad, Nayef Aguerd, and Ismael Saibari all dealing with fitness concerns ahead of the May 26 squad announcement.

Mazraoui Shoulder Subluxation Risk

Noussair Mazraoui could miss Morocco's debut against Brazil due to shoulder subluxation, with Youssef Belammari set to take over the starting role at left-back, further weakening that flank.

⚽ Recent Form & Pre-Tournament Results

Latest Friendly: 1–1 vs Norway (Jun 7)

Martin Ødegaard's second-half strike cancelled out an early Brahim Díaz goal as Norway drew 1-1 with Morocco in each team's final game before the World Cup. Morocco had 46% possession and 5 shots on goal to Norway's 2.

Recent Warm-Up Performances

Morocco's pre-tournament record included victories: 5–0 vs Burundi (May 26), 4–0 vs Madagascar (Jun 2), before the 1–1 draw with Norway. Morocco head into World Cup 2026 as Africa's in-form side, aiming to build on their historic semi-final run.

Group C: Brazil, Scotland, Haiti

Group C of the 2026 FIFA World Cup will take place from June 13 to 24, 2026, and consists of Brazil, Morocco, Haiti, and Scotland. The top two teams, possibly along with the third-placed team, will advance to the round of 32.

👥 Squad & Roster Status

Final 26-Man Squad Announced May 26

Morocco announced their final squad on May 26. Morocco planned a preparatory camp from May 22–26 involving players from the provisional World Cup pool, with sessions including all available pre-selected players as the coach finalized decisions. Morocco also scheduled a closed-door friendly against Burundi during that period.

Key Players & Leadership

Key figures such as Achraf Hakimi, Sofyan Amrabat, Azzedine Ounahi, and Nayef Aguerd remain crucial to the squad's ambitions. Morocco head into World Cup 2026 as Africa's in-form side, aiming to build on their historic semi-final run with Hakimi leading a talented, disciplined squad.

Attacking Options Post-Ezzalzouli

Without Abde Ezzalzouli, most of Morocco's attacking responsibilities would fall to Brahim Diaz. Although he does not possess the same change of pace, the 26-year-old star has proven capable of orchestrating the entire national team attack and leads the team in goal scoring.

🎯 Tactical Profile & Expectations

Experienced Defensive Lineup

The squad saw Jawad El Yamiq and goalkeeper El Mehdi Benabid returning after missing previous international matches. Their comeback strengthens the team's depth, particularly in defense and goalkeeping. Morocco's defensive approach proved resilient during qualifying, winning CAF Group E.

Historic Credentials & 2022 Momentum

After Morocco's historic 2022 FIFA World Cup semi-final run, expectations remain high, and Regragui has maintained a balance between experienced leaders and emerging talents. Despite being among the favorites to surprise once again at the 2026 World Cup, they have undergone major changes within the national team that have halted their sporting rise.

Brazil Challenge & Group Difficulty

Facing Brazil, Scotland, and Haiti in the 2026 World Cup, Abde's absence could seriously limit Morocco's chances. Without his change of pace and dribbling ability, they could struggle to impose their dominant style of play. Brazil historically dominates this matchup.
